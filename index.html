Engineering telemetry

Total cost—

Every system draws from one budget (game balance).

Liftoff thrust-to-weight—

Below 1.0, or with no working engine, the rocket stays on the pad and the mission fails.

Cruise power—

Generation / draw. Engine pumps only draw power during the ascent and are left out.

Δv to orbit—

Sum of every stage; each one drops away when empty. Target: low Earth orbit ≈ 9,400 m/s ⚠.

Mars transfer—

Reach low Earth orbit first.
